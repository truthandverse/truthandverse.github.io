Privacy Policy – Truth & Verse publishing tool

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This is a private tool used only by the owner of the Truth & Verse channel to publish the channel's own videos to its own TikTok account.

What data we access. When the account owner logs in with TikTok, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name and avatar) for that single account. It uploads only videos created by the channel.

How we use it. The token is used only to upload and publish the channel's videos and to check the status of each upload. We do not access other users' data, we do not read messages, and we do not collect data from viewers.

Storage. The token is stored only on the owner's personal computer. It is never sold, shared or sent to third parties.

Deleting data. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services) and delete the local token file.

Contact: send a direct message to @truthverse_brasil on Instagram.
